## The main difference is the work surface

Notion is a workspace for documents, databases, projects and shared knowledge. First Light is a personal planner for commitments and daily follow-through. Both can hold tasks; the decision is about where your work belongs, not whether one product can think and the other cannot.

Choose Notion when the task needs to stay close to substantial documentation, connected databases or a team's shared workspace. Choose First Light when you want dedicated Today, Inbox and upcoming views alongside notes, a calendar, habits and focus sessions.

## Both offer AI connections

Notion provides an official hosted MCP server that lets authorized clients read and update accessible content. Earlier claims on this blog that Notion has no MCP support were incorrect.

First Light offers API/MCP access on Pro. Compatible clients can read and edit tasks after setup. Planning tools return suggestions; an editing call is needed to save a change. MCP access alone does not guarantee that every client supports the connection or that the assistant will make the right decision.

## What First Light's AI does

Brief me summarizes recorded commitments and suggested next actions on request. Plan my day suggests priorities and urgency/importance quadrants. It does not automatically create a complete calendar schedule or know everything in your other apps. Voice/image capture and weekly reviews are Pro features.

## Use one source for each commitment

If your team already works in Notion, moving every shared task into a second app may create more work. You can keep project documentation there and use First Light for your own next actions, but decide which record owns each deadline. There is no automatic Notion-to-First Light synchronization promised here.

Try a small set of real commitments before migrating. Check current prices, permissions and supported workflows rather than choosing from an old feature matrix.

Sources: [Notion product](https://www.notion.com/product), [Notion MCP](https://developers.notion.com/guides/mcp/overview).

## Using First Light

The core task planner is free. **Brief me** is optional and uses recorded tasks and calendar context; it does not change your tasks. Plus adds Google Calendar editing and reflection. Pro adds planning suggestions, voice/image capture, weekly reviews and API/MCP access. AI features have daily usage limits. Saving, syncing and AI require internet access.
