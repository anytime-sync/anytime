## What changed

Early descriptions of First Light centered on an automatically delivered Daily Edition. The current product puts personal task management first and offers **Brief me** when you want help reviewing your commitments. You can capture, organize and complete work without an AI briefing.

## What the briefing uses

Brief me reads a bounded set of your open tasks and upcoming calendar events saved in First Light. It returns suggested next actions with links to the source records and flags missing context. It does not independently read your entire email inbox, verify business results or measure your energy.

Check the source behind a suggestion before acting. An absent meeting record does not prove you are free, and a valid link does not guarantee that an AI interpretation is correct. If the source is incomplete, improve the task or calendar entry.

## What it changes

Generating a briefing does not change task dates or create time blocks. It gives you material for a decision. Editing a task or asking a configured assistant to make an edit is a separate step.

Daily Edition is still a name used in older articles and some interfaces. It should not be read as a promise of automatic morning delivery. Email digests, notifications and the Morning Co-pilot have separate settings and behavior.

## What we can substantiate

We have removed the previous claim of an 87% open rate, an unverified customer quote and a guaranteed generation time. Those statements were not supported by evidence available for this review. We also removed roadmap items presented as if delivery were committed.

The practical test is whether the brief helps you choose and complete a useful next action. More generated text is not, by itself, proof that the product is helping.
