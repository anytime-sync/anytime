## Start with one repeatable action

A morning routine does not need to be a second job. Choose a small action you can repeat even on a busy day: open your planner, check fixed appointments, and choose the next task. You can add exercise, reading or other routines separately if they fit your life.

The useful question is not “Have I completed the ideal routine?” It is “Do I know what I am doing next?”

## A practical First Light routine

1. Open Today and check your recorded calendar events.
2. Capture anything you are still holding in your head.
3. Choose one task to start. Give it a specific next action.
4. If you need help reviewing the list, request **Brief me**.
5. Check the source records behind any suggestion before acting.

The briefing is optional. It does not need to run overnight, and First Light does not promise a scheduled AI edition at a chosen morning time. Email digests and reminders are separate features with their own settings.

## Keep the routine useful

A habit tracker can record the routines you choose, but a streak does not tell you whether the routine is helping. After a week, ask whether you started meaningful work with less effort. If the ritual takes longer than the task, shorten it.

Do not ask the app to infer your energy from an empty calendar. Record exercise or other fixed commitments when they affect availability, and leave room for travel and rest. Keep the planner accurate enough to help you make a decision, rather than adding detail you will not maintain.
