## Start with a reliable list

AI can help you review work, but it cannot account for commitments you have never recorded. Capture the task, its real deadline and enough context to decide what comes next. Connect Google Calendar if you want its recorded events alongside your tasks. Check that the events have synced before relying on that view.

Separate a deadline from the time you intend to work. A report due Friday may need attention on Tuesday. Moving its work session should not become an excuse to forget the actual deadline.

## Ask for a proposal, then decide

In First Light, **Plan my day** suggests priorities and urgency/importance quadrants using your tasks and recorded calendar context. It is not a conversational scheduler and does not automatically create focus blocks. Review the suggestions and apply the changes you choose. The Morning Co-pilot is a separate Pro feature for follow-up questions.

Choose one task you must finish, one you want to advance, and one you can deliberately defer. If a suggestion ignores an important constraint, keep your judgment and correct the underlying information.

## Leave room for reality

Check appointments, travel, breaks and work that is not in the app. A gap in the calendar is not proof that you are available. Reserve some space for interruptions rather than filling every minute.

At the end of the day, compare the work you recorded as complete with what remains. Change the next action when the old one is too vague. The useful output is a decision you can act on, not a perfect-looking plan.
